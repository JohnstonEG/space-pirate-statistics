# Space Pirate Statistics

Play it at **https://johnstoneg.github.io/space-pirate-statistics/**

You're an accountant kidnapped by space pirates. The captain wants numbers before every raid:
odds, intervals, test results. Work each job with the tables, calculator and reference
sheets on your monitors, give the quartermaster your answers, and make the call. Keep
your accuracy up and you might end up running the ship. Miss too much and you go out
the airlock.

Built for ECO 138. Runs in any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

## The goal

Clear every order (topic) in your chosen course. An A in accuracy (90% or better) makes you
captain of the Starry Seas. Finish below an A and the crew lets you go. Three fully missed
jobs in a row puts you overboard.

## Rules

- The clock starts when a job opens. Faster correct answers earn more plunder.
- Answers marked **CALC** must be worked on the in-game calculator and sent with Tab and
  the slot number. Don't round them.
- Typed probabilities take four decimal places (Table A.1 values take three).
- Copying and pasting are off.
- Leaving the game window during a job, whether by switching tabs or apps, minimizing,
  or using a screenshot tool, loses that target and gives you a new one of the same kind.
  Between jobs, take as long as you like.
- Practice mode has no clock and doesn't count. True mode is the scored run.
- At the end, download your results zip and submit it unchanged on Canvas.
